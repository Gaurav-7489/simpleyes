About

Redefining consular services, simply

Simplyes exists to make Indian consular and administrative processes easier to understand for the Indian community in Germany — with clear explanations, careful preparation and honest boundaries.

Talk to Simplyes (opens WhatsApp in a new tab)Our Services

Simplyes at a glance

Official procedures can be detailed and change over time. Many people simply want someone to explain what applies to them and help them prepare properly. That's the gap Simplyes fills — as a private service that is transparent about what it can and cannot do.

What Simplyes is
An independent private assistance service.
Who we help
Indians and people of Indian origin living in Germany.
What we help with
Understanding Indian consular and administrative requirements, documents, forms, photos, signatures and next steps.
Where
We assist customers across Germany.
How to reach us
WhatsApp +49 176 62510556.
What we don't do
Issue documents, approve applications, guarantee appointments or speed up official processing.

Transparency

Independent support. Clear boundaries.

Simplyes is a private assistance service. Official applications, decisions, approvals, and processing are handled by the respective government authorities or designated service providers. We help customers understand requirements and prepare for their next steps.

Ask Us (opens WhatsApp in a new tab)

We help you

  • Explain requirements and procedures in plain language
  • Help check whether your documents appear complete
  • Support preparing and reviewing forms
  • Format photos and signatures to stated requirements
  • Help you prepare for appointments and next steps

Authorities

Official bodies & their service providers

  • Set the official requirements and fees
  • Accept and process applications
  • Issue passports, visas and official documents
  • Manage appointments and processing times
  • Make all approvals and decisions

For official requirements, fees and announcements, always refer to the Embassy of India, Berlin (official website, opens in a new tab) and the official channels it lists.
